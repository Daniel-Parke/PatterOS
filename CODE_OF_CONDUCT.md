# Code of conduct

This project exists to help people who have been told, one way or another, that
this sort of thing is not for them. So the standard here is simple.

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are talking to is doing their best with what
they know.

## What that means in practice

- **No question is too basic.** Someone asking what a terminal is has exactly as
  much right to be here as someone sending a patch. If a question seems obvious
  to you, that is a gap in our documentation, not a failing in the person
  asking.
- **Explain, do not perform.** Answers that show off are worth less than answers
  that land.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** "That will break on an 8 GB card,
  here is why" is welcome. Making someone feel stupid is not.
- **Assume good faith, and say when you are unsure.** "I think this is wrong but
  I have not tested it" is a genuinely useful contribution.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments about anyone's background,
identity or level of experience. Publishing someone's private information.
Persistently pushing past a "no" after a decision has been made and explained.

## If something goes wrong

Contact Daniel Parke, through this repository or the channel. Reports are
handled privately.

Anything that is unwelcome will be dealt with proportionately: usually a word,
occasionally an edit or a lock, and removal from the project if it comes to
that. The aim is to keep this a place where a beginner will risk asking, not to
run a disciplinary process.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing PatterOS.
